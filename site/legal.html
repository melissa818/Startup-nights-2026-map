Privacy policy

Version of 5 November 2026 (updated for the AI assistant)

This privacy policy explains how personal data is processed in the “Startup Nights 2026 booth map” web app (the “app”), including its AI assistant. It follows the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP). If the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies to you, this policy covers it too.

1. Who is responsible

Langrove is responsible for processing your data (the “controller”). For privacy questions and requests, contact us at melissa@tothemoon.build.

The organisers of Startup Nights, the Swiss Startup Association and the exhibitors do not operate the app and receive no personal data from it.

2. In short

  • You can browse the map and use the AI assistant without an account.
  • Questions you ask the AI assistant are sent to our AI provider, Anthropic, to produce an answer. We do not store your questions or the answers.
  • Feedback you send is stored and read only by the Langrove team.
  • With an account we process your email address. Your marks and notes are private: only you can see them.
  • No advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, no selling of data.
  • All data is deleted automatically on 6 December 2026, or earlier when you delete your account.

3. What we process and why

  • Technical data whenever you use the app: IP address, date and time, the page or file requested, browser and device information. These appear in the server logs of our hosting. Purpose: to deliver the app, keep it secure and fix errors.
  • AI assistant: your question and the last few messages of your conversation, so the assistant understands follow-up questions. They are sent to our server and to Anthropic to produce the answer, then discarded. We do not store them in our database. The conversation stays only in your browser tab and disappears when you close it. To prevent abuse and keep costs in check, we record when a question was asked, together with a random ID stored in your browser (and your account, if you are signed in), but not the question itself. Our hosting also records technical data about each AI request, such as model, size and duration, for operation and billing. Purpose: to answer your questions about the event and the map.
  • Feedback you send with the form or through the assistant: your message, an optional rating, an optional name or email if you want a reply, the time, and whether it came from the form or the assistant. If you are signed in, it is linked to your account. Purpose: to improve the app and our products. Only the Langrove team can read it.
  • Sign-in: your email address, one-time sign-in codes (valid 10 minutes) and a session cookie. Purpose: to sign you in and keep you signed in. We do not use your email address for newsletters or marketing.
  • Profile: the booth you exhibit at (only if you tell us), whether you have seen the welcome message, and when you accepted these terms. Purpose: to run your account and record your acceptance.
  • Private content: booths you mark as visited or starred, your notes on startups, your general notes and, for exhibitors, notes about people who visited their booth. Purpose: your own planning at the event. Only you can read them.
  • Data stored in your browser: settings such as whether you have seen a tip, a copy of your marks and notes so the app works with a weak connection (removed when you sign out), the random ID mentioned above, and the conversation with the assistant in the current tab. This data is not used for tracking.

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on: providing the app and assistant you asked to use (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR); our legitimate interests in security, preventing abuse and improving the app (Art. 6(1)(f)); and legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c)).

During testing, the app briefly had a public chat. It has been removed. Its messages are no longer shown and are deleted by 6 December 2026 at the latest.

4. Who receives data

  • Anthropic, PBC (USA) receives your questions to the AI assistant and the recent conversation, and produces the answers with its Claude model. It processes them on our behalf.
  • Our hosting and infrastructure. The app runs on Langrove’s platform, hosted by Amazon Web Services (AWS) in the European Union (Ireland). AWS stores the data and server logs. Sign-in codes are sent by email through our platform’s email service. These providers process data only on our behalf.
  • The Langrove team reads feedback.
  • Authorities or other parties, where we are legally required to share data or need it to protect our rights.

We do not sell or rent personal data and do not share it for advertising.

5. Data abroad

Data is stored in the European Union (Ireland). Switzerland recognises the EU as providing adequate data protection. Questions to the AI assistant are processed by Anthropic in the USA, and AWS is part of a US group. For transfers to the USA we rely on recognised safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses or the Swiss–U.S. Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified under it. Under its commercial terms, Anthropic does not use these requests to train its models and keeps them only for a limited time, for example to detect abuse.

6. How long we keep data

  • Everything is deleted automatically on 6 December 2026, 30 days after the event. This covers accounts, marks, notes, feedback and the records of when questions were asked.
  • Earlier if you delete your account (account window → “Delete my account”). This happens immediately and includes feedback you sent while signed in.
  • Questions to the AI assistant are not stored by us. The conversation in your browser disappears when you close the tab.
  • Sign-in codes expire after 10 minutes. Server logs are kept by our hosting for a short time for security and operation.

7. Security

All connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Access rules ensure that only you can read your private data and only the Langrove team can read feedback. The session cookie cannot be read by scripts. No system is completely secure, so please do not enter sensitive information.

8. Your rights

Under the FADP (and the GDPR, if it applies) you can ask for information about the data we hold about you, have incorrect data corrected, have data deleted, object to processing, receive your data in a common format, and withdraw consent you gave.

In the app you can already export your marks and notes (“Export my notes”) and delete your account with all its data. For anything else, write to melissa@tothemoon.build. We answer within 30 days and may ask you to confirm your identity.

You can complain to the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC), Feldeggweg 1, 3003 Bern, www.edoeb.admin.ch. In the EU, you can also contact your local data protection authority.

9. Cookies and tracking

The app sets one technically necessary cookie: the session cookie once you sign in. The random ID used for the assistant’s limits stays in your browser’s storage and is not used for tracking or shared. There are no advertising or analytics cookies, no third-party trackers and no external fonts. Links you open from the app, such as startup websites or WhatsApp sharing, are covered by those sites’ own privacy policies.

10. No automated decisions

The AI assistant answers questions about the event. It makes no decisions about you.

11. Age

The app is meant for people aged 16 or older. Younger people should only create an account or use the AI assistant with permission from a parent or guardian.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. The version date at the top shows the current version. If we make significant changes, we ask signed-in users to accept them again.

AI assistant

Version of 5 November 2026

What it does

  • Tells you where a startup, a booth number or a place (stages, workshops, bars, food, toilets, entrance, exits) is, and shows it on the map.
  • Finds startups by topic, for example “Which startups work on robotics?”.
  • Passes your feedback on the app to the Langrove team. Just tell it, or use the feedback form.

It uses Claude, an AI model by Anthropic. It only knows the booth list and floor plan of this map. It does not know the event programme, ticket details or anything that happens on site.

It can be wrong

AI answers can be inaccurate or incomplete. Booth positions come from the organisers’ floor plan, and startup descriptions from public web sources. Check the map and the signs on site.

Your privacy

  • Don’t share personal or confidential information, such as names with contact details, health data, passwords or business secrets.
  • Your questions are sent to Anthropic in the USA to produce the answer. We do not store your questions or the answers.
  • The conversation stays in your browser tab until you close it.
  • Feedback you send is stored privately for the Langrove team and deleted on 6 December 2026. Only add your name or email if you want a reply.

Limits

To keep this free demo running for everyone, there is a limit on how many questions one browser and all users together can ask per minute.
