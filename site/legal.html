Privacy policy

Version of 5 November 2026

This privacy policy explains how personal data is processed in the “Startup Nights 2026 booth map” web app (the “app”), including its live chat. It follows the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP). If the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies to you, this policy covers it too.

1. Who is responsible

Langrove is responsible for processing your data (the “controller”). For privacy questions and requests, and to report chat content, contact us at melissa@tothemoon.build.

The organisers of Startup Nights, the Swiss Startup Association and the exhibitors do not operate the app and receive no personal data from it.

2. In short

  • You can browse the map without an account. We then only process the technical data needed to deliver the app.
  • With an account we process your email address and what you create in the app.
  • Your marks and notes are private: only you can see them. Chat messages, your chat name and photos you post in the chat are public.
  • No advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, no selling of data.
  • All data is deleted automatically on 6 December 2026, or earlier when you delete your account.

3. What we process and why

  • Technical data whenever you use the app: IP address, date and time, the page or file requested, browser and device information. These appear in the server logs of our hosting. Purpose: to deliver the app, keep it secure and fix errors.
  • Sign-in: your email address, one-time sign-in codes (valid 10 minutes) and a session cookie. Purpose: to sign you in and keep you signed in. We do not use your email address for newsletters or marketing and never show it to other users.
  • Profile: your chat name, the booth you exhibit at (only if you tell us), whether you have seen the welcome message, and when you accepted these terms. Purpose: to run your account, show your chat name and record your acceptance.
  • Private content: booths you mark as visited or starred, your notes on startups, your general notes and, for exhibitors, notes about people who visited their booth. Purpose: your own planning at the event. Only you can read them.
  • Public chat: your messages, photos you post (and whatever and whoever they show), your chat name and when you posted. Purpose: a public chat for people at the event.
  • Reports: which chat message you reported, the reason and your optional note. Purpose: moderation and preventing abuse.
  • Data stored in your browser: settings such as whether you have seen a tip, a copy of your marks and notes so the app works with a weak connection (removed when you sign out), and which chat messages you have already seen. This data stays on your device and is not used for tracking.

Before uploading a photo, the app shrinks it and removes hidden details stored in the file, such as GPS location and camera data. It does not remove anything visible in the photo.

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on: providing the app you asked to use (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR); our legitimate interests in security, moderation and preventing abuse (Art. 6(1)(f)); and legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c)).

4. The live chat is public

Everything you post in the live chat (text, photos, chat name and time) can be seen by anyone who opens the app, including people who are not at the event and people without an account. Others can take screenshots, copy or share it, and we cannot control that. When you delete a message, it is removed from the app but not from copies other people made.

Please do not post personal data (yours or anyone else’s), confidential information, or photos of people without their consent. See the chat rules.

5. Who receives data

  • Other users see your public chat content, and nothing else.
  • Our hosting and infrastructure. The app runs on Langrove’s platform, hosted by Amazon Web Services (AWS) in the European Union (Ireland). AWS stores the data, files and server logs and delivers live chat updates. Sign-in codes are sent by email through our platform’s email service. These providers process data only on our behalf.
  • Authorities or other parties, where we are legally required to share data or need it to protect our rights, for example in the case of illegal content.

We do not sell or rent personal data and do not share it for advertising.

6. Data abroad

Data is stored in the European Union (Ireland). Switzerland recognises the EU as providing adequate data protection. AWS is part of a US group. If data is accessed from the USA or from another country without adequate protection, this relies on recognised safeguards such as the Swiss–U.S. Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

7. How long we keep data

  • Everything is deleted automatically on 6 December 2026, 30 days after the event. This covers accounts, marks, notes, chat messages, photos and reports.
  • Earlier if you delete your account (account window → “Delete my account”). This happens immediately and includes your chat messages and photos.
  • A chat message you delete, or that a host removes, loses its text and photo immediately.
  • Sign-in codes expire after 10 minutes. Server logs are kept by our hosting for a short time for security and operation.
  • Data stored in your browser stays there until you sign out or clear it.

8. Security

All connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Access rules ensure that only you can read your private data, and the session cookie cannot be read by scripts. No system is completely secure, so please do not store or post sensitive information.

9. Your rights

Under the FADP (and the GDPR, if it applies) you can ask for information about the data we hold about you, have incorrect data corrected, have data deleted, object to processing, receive your data in a common format, and withdraw consent you gave.

In the app you can already export your marks and notes (“Export my notes”), delete your chat messages, change your chat name, and delete your account with all its data. For anything else, write to melissa@tothemoon.build. We answer within 30 days and may ask you to confirm your identity.

You can complain to the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC), Feldeggweg 1, 3003 Bern, www.edoeb.admin.ch. In the EU, you can also contact your local data protection authority.

10. Cookies and tracking

The app sets one technically necessary cookie: the session cookie once you sign in. There are no advertising or analytics cookies, no third-party trackers and no external fonts. Links you open from the app, such as startup websites or WhatsApp sharing, are covered by those sites’ own privacy policies.

11. Automated removal of reported messages

A chat message reported by three different users is hidden automatically. If you think this happened to a message of yours by mistake, contact us and we will review it.

12. Age

The app is meant for people aged 16 or older. Younger people should only create an account or use the chat with permission from a parent or guardian.

13. Changes

We may update this policy. The version date at the top shows the current version. If we make significant changes, we ask signed-in users to accept them again.

Chat rules

Version of 5 November 2026

The live chat is public. Anyone who opens the app can read it, from anywhere and without an account. People can take screenshots. Post only what you are happy for anyone to see.

Welcome

  • Tips, questions and meet-ups about the event
  • Photos of booths, talks and the venue that you are allowed to share
  • Friendly, respectful conversation

Do not post

  • Personal data of others without their consent: names with contact details, phone numbers, email or home addresses, badge details. Think twice about your own.
  • Confidential information: trade secrets, anything under an NDA, unreleased products, financial or investor information, passwords or access codes. This includes photos of screens, slides, documents, whiteboards or name badges that show such information.
  • Photos of people without asking them first. In Switzerland everyone has a right to their own image (Art. 28 Swiss Civil Code). No photos of children unless a parent agrees.
  • Insults, defamation, threats, harassment, discrimination or hate (for example under Art. 173 ff. and Art. 261bis Swiss Criminal Code).
  • Sexual, violent or otherwise illegal content, or content that infringes copyrights or trademarks.
  • Spam, chain messages, scams, phishing or harmful links.
  • Pretending to be someone else, for example a host, the organisers or a startup.

Before you post a photo

  • Did everyone recognisable in it agree?
  • Check the background for screens, documents, badges and other people.
  • Only post photos you took yourself or are allowed to share.

If something goes wrong

  • Posted something by mistake? Delete it right away with the bin icon next to your message.
  • See something that breaks these rules? Report it with the flag icon next to the message, or email melissa@tothemoon.build. A message reported by three people is hidden automatically.
  • Messages with a yellow “Host” badge come from the app’s hosts, who can remove any message.
